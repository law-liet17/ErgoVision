Pipeline

How a photo becomes a score

01

Pose landmarks

MediaPipe finds 33 body points in the image and in metric 3D. Pixel coordinates are rescaled by width and height so the aspect ratio cannot skew an angle.

02

Worksheet angles

Trunk vs gravity; neck and upper arm vs the trunk axis; elbow and knee as flexion from straight. Signs are kept, so extension never masquerades as flexion.

03

Chart lookups

Each angle lands in its band, adjustments are added, then Table A, B and C of RULA and REBA are read exactly as printed. Load, grip and repetition come from you.

04

Ranked fixes

Every finding is tied to the angle and worksheet row that produced it, and sorted by the component score — the biggest number is the cheapest place to start.
